00Start here — 怎麼用這個網站

為什麼「看詳解」學不起來？ 看得懂別人的解答，跟自己做得出來，是兩種不同的能力。詳解一次全攤開，大腦只是在「讀」，沒有在「想」。所以這個網站的詳解預設一次只給一步：看完一步，先自己猜下一步，再按「下一步」對答案。

Loop每一題都照這個順序

  1. 先自己做。例題在表格裡填數字；選擇題直接選。不會也先猜一個，猜錯比空白學得多。
  2. 按 Check 對答案。綠色是對的，紅色的再想一次。選擇題答錯會出現提示，可以再選，不會直接告訴你答案。
  3. 真的卡住才看詳解，一次一步。每看完一步，先蓋住，想想下一步要算什麼，再按「下一步 ▸」。
  4. 看完詳解後，按 Reset / 再做一次，自己從頭做一遍。這一步最重要，做得出來才算學會。
  5. 答錯的選擇題會自動進 錯題本。隔天再做一次，第一次就答對才會從錯題本移除。

Map整門課其實只有一條線

交易 Transaction→分錄 Journal entry→期末調整 Adjusting→損益表 Income statement→保留盈餘 Retained earnings→財務狀況表 SFP→現金流量表 Cash flows

每一關都是這條線上的一小段：存貨（第 4 關）決定 COGS；應收帳款（第 5 關）決定呆帳費用；設備（第 6 關）決定折舊與處分損益；負債（第 7 關）決定利息；第 8 關學現金流量表。期中考 Part B（存貨、設備折舊、更正損益表或現金流量表，共 50%）的兩年題型都在第 9 關用考試格式練。所以讀每個單元時都問自己：這個數字最後會出現在哪張表的哪一行？

Path路線圖：一關一關來，最後做得出綜合題

整個網站已經照學習順序排好，分成 10 關。每一關開頭會告訴你這關的目標、照順序要做哪些事、過關條件。預設是專心模式：畫面只顯示目前這一關，不會被後面的內容嚇到。想看全部，按最上面的「專心模式」切換。每關最後把單元的「完成」勾起來，就會出現「進下一關」。不一定要照順序，但建議照順序：後面每一關都用到前面的東西。

    02The Accounting Equation & Double-entry Bookkeeping

    DefinitionsKey terms

    Assets
    Resources a business owns ("something you own"). e.g. Cash, Accounts Receivable, Supplies, Equipment.資產：公司擁有的資源。
    Liabilities
    Claims of creditors against assets ("something you owe"). e.g. Accounts Payable, Notes Payable, Salaries and Wages Payable.負債：欠債權人的。
    Equity
    Ownership claim of shareholders ("invested in you and something you have earned"). Equity = Share Capital—Ordinary + Retained Earnings.權益：股東的部分 = 股本 + 保留盈餘。
    Retained Earnings
    = Revenues − Expenses − Dividends (accumulated).保留盈餘：累積的收入減費用減股利。
    Assets = Liabilities + Equity → this is the Statement of Financial Position (Balance Sheet). Revenues − Expenses → the Income Statement.上面那條等式對應資產負債表；收入減費用對應損益表。收入讓權益↑，費用和股利讓權益↓。

    ExampleAsset, liability, or equity?

    Indicate whether each of the following items is an asset (A), liability (L), or part of equity (E).

    詳解 Solution

    a. Accounts receivable — Asset (customers owe you cash)
    b. Salaries and wages payable — Liability (you owe employees)
    c. Equipment — Asset
    d. Supplies — Asset (owned, not yet used)
    e. Share capital—ordinary — Equity (shareholders' investment)
    f. Notes payable — Liability (written promise to repay)

    看字尾：-receivable 是別人欠你（資產）；-payable 是你欠別人（負債）。Supplies 是還沒用掉的資產，用掉才變 Supplies Expense。

    ReviewThe four financial statements (TA 9/24)

    StatementShowsPart of the equation

    編製順序：損益表 → 保留盈餘表（需要淨利）→ 資產負債表（需要期末保留盈餘）→ 現金流量表。

    On which financial statement does each item first appear?

    詳解 Solution

    Revenues and expenses → income statement. Dividends → retained earnings statement (they reduce RE; they are not an expense). Accounts payable and equipment → statement of financial position. Cash paid to buy equipment → statement of cash flows.

    最常錯：Dividends 不在損益表上，在保留盈餘表。

    Mnemonic DEA-LER: Dividends, Expenses, Assets have a normal debit balance; Liabilities, Equity, Revenues have a normal credit balance. In every entry, total debits = total credits.口訣 DEALER：DEA 在借方、LER 在貸方。每筆分錄借貸相等。

    A compound journal entry has two or more debits and/or credits in a single transaction (e.g. paying several expenses in one payment).複合分錄：一筆交易有兩個以上的借或貸。

    MethodHow to record any transaction

    1. What was received or given up? Identify at least two accounts affected.拿到或付出了什麼？找出受影響的帳戶。
    2. Classify each account: A / L / E / Revenue / Expense / Dividend.判斷每個帳戶屬於哪一類。
    3. Increase or decrease? Use the table to decide Dr or Cr.增加或減少，用上表決定借或貸。
    4. Check: debits = credits, and the equation still balances.檢查借貸相等、等式平衡。

    03Debit & Credit Gym 借貸特訓

    Debits and credits are not something to memorize as a list. They follow from one equation. Learn the reason once, then drill until it is automatic.借貸不是硬背的，是從會計等式推出來的。先懂一次「為什麼」，再用下面兩個練習場練到不用想。

    Why借＝左邊，貸＝右邊，從等式推出來

    Assets 資產在等號左邊
    增加記 借 Dr（左）
    減少記 貸 Cr
    =
    Liabilities 負債在等號右邊
    增加記 貸 Cr（右）
    減少記 借 Dr
    +
    Equity 權益在等號右邊
    增加記 貸 Cr（右）
    減少記 借 Dr
    • Revenues 收入 讓權益 ↑ → 跟權益同方向 → 增加記 貸
    • Expenses 費用、Dividends 股利 讓權益 ↓ → 跟權益反方向 → 增加記 借

    Debit 只是「左邊」、credit 只是「右邊」，跟好壞無關，也不是「錢進來／錢出去」的意思。每一筆分錄借方總額一定等於貸方總額，所以等式永遠平衡。口訣 DEA-LER：Dividends、Expenses、Assets 增加記借；Liabilities、Equity、Revenues 增加記貸。上面的圖就是這個口訣的原因。

    Method每一筆分錄都問三個問題

    1. 哪兩個科目動了？問自己：公司拿到了什麼？付出了什麼、或欠了誰？
    2. 每個科目是哪一類？增加還是減少？看字尾：-Payable 負債、-Expense 費用、-Revenue 收入、-Receivable 資產、Prepaid 資產、Unearned 負債。
    3. 套規則：資產、費用、股利增加 → 借；負債、權益、收入增加 → 貸；減少就反過來。最後檢查一借一貸。
    新手捷徑：先找 Cash。 收到現金 → 借 Cash；付出現金 → 貸 Cash。現金佔掉一邊，另一個科目就在另一邊，你只需要想它叫什麼名字。沒有現金的交易（賒購、賒銷、調整分錄）才需要完整走三個問題。
    最常見的五個誤會
    1. 「收入是好事，所以記借」✗ → 收入讓權益增加，記貸。
    2. 「付錢給供應商就是費用」✗ → 付應付帳款是負債減少（借 A/P），費用早在賒購時就記了。
    3. 「Prepaid Insurance 是費用」✗ → 預付是資產（還沒用掉的權利），過期了才轉成 Insurance Expense。
    4. 「Unearned Revenue 是收入」✗ → 預收是負債（欠客戶服務），做完才轉成 Revenue。
    5. 「Salaries Payable 跟 Salaries Expense 一樣」✗ → Payable 是負債（還沒付），Expense 是費用（已發生）。

    Gym 1科目卡：這是哪一類？增加記哪邊？

    看到科目名稱，先選類別，再選「增加時記借還是貸」（也就是正常餘額）。答錯會給提示。目標：連續答對 20 張。

    04Adjusting Entries

    DefinitionsAccrual vs. cash basis

    Accrual-basis accounting
    Companies record transactions in the periods in which the events occur (required by IFRS).權責發生制：事件發生的期間就記錄，不管現金何時收付。IFRS 要求。
    Cash-basis accounting
    Record revenue when cash is received and an expense when cash is paid.現金制：收到錢才記收入、付錢才記費用。
    Adjusting entries
    Entries made at the end of an accounting period to ensure that companies follow the revenue recognition and expense recognition principles.調整分錄：期末為了符合收入與費用認列原則而做的分錄。

    DEFERRALS（遞延：先收付現金）

    • Prepaid expenses (asset) → expire over time
      Dr Expense / Cr Prepaid ___預付費用（資產），用掉轉費用
    • Unearned revenues (liability) → earned over time
      Dr Unearned ___ Revenue / Cr ___ Revenue預收收入（負債），做完轉收入

    ACCRUALS（應計：還沒收付現金）

    • Accrued expenses: expenses incurred but not yet paid in cash
      Dr Expense / Cr ___ Payable應計費用，例如欠薪、利息
    • Accrued revenues: revenues for services performed but not yet received in cash
      Dr ___ Receivable / Cr Revenue應計收入，做了服務還沒收錢
    Pattern: every adjusting entry involves one income statement account and one statement of financial position account, and never Cash.每筆調整分錄 = 一個損益表科目 + 一個資產負債表科目，且不會動到 Cash。

    MethodComputing the adjustment

    1. Monthly amount = total ÷ number of months.每月金額 = 總額 ÷ 月數
    2. Count months elapsed in this period (start date → year-end).算本期經過幾個月
    3. Monthly amount × months = expense (or revenue) for this period.相乘得本期費用或收入
    4. The remainder is the ending Prepaid (or Unearned) balance.剩下的是期末餘額

    ExampleMajor Ltd. (the payer)

    On July 1, 2020, Major Ltd. pays £15,120 to Orlow Insurance for a 3-year insurance contract. Both companies have fiscal years ending December 31. For Major Ltd., journalize and post the entry on July 1 and the annual adjusting entry on December 31.

    詳解 Solution

    Step 1 — 15,120 ÷ 3 = 5,040 per year; 5,040 ÷ 12 = 420 per month.
    Step 2 — July 1 to Dec 31 = 6 months expired.
    Step 3 — 420 × 6 = £2,520 insurance expense.注意：是 6 個月不是 12 個月，從 7/1 才開始。

    Jul 1 Prepaid Insurance15,120
    Cash15,120
    Dec 31 Insurance Expense2,520
    Prepaid Insurance2,520

    Prepaid Insurance balance at Dec 31 = £12,600.

    Q: What if Major Ltd. neglects to make the year-end adjustment for the expired insurance?
    Expenses understated by £2,520 → net income overstated by £2,520; Prepaid Insurance not reduced → assets overstated by £2,520.忘了調整：淨利高估、資產高估各 2,520。

    ExampleOrlow Insurance (the receiver)

    Using the previous data, journalize the entry on July 1 and the adjusting entry on December 31 for Orlow Insurance. Orlow uses Unearned Service Revenue and Service Revenue.

    詳解 Solution

    Orlow received cash before performing the service → it owes coverage → liability (Unearned Service Revenue). Each month of coverage provided earns £420; 6 months → £2,520 moves from the liability to revenue.收款方先收錢、還沒提供服務 → 負債。過了 6 個月就賺到 2,520。

    Jul 1 Cash15,120
    Unearned Service Revenue15,120
    Dec 31 Unearned Service Revenue2,520
    Service Revenue2,520

    Unearned Service Revenue balance at Dec 31 = £12,600 (liability).

    Q: What if Orlow neglects the year-end adjustment for the earned portion?
    Revenue understated → net income understated by £2,520; Unearned not reduced → liabilities overstated by £2,520.忘了調整：淨利低估、負債高估各 2,520。

    ExampleSupplies — the most-tested adjustment

    Supplies are bought as an asset. At period end the company counts what is left; the part used up becomes Supplies Expense.

    Supplies expense = Beginning supplies + Purchases − Supplies on hand at the end. Adjusting entry: Dr Supplies Expense / Cr Supplies.用品費用＝期初＋本期買進－期末盤點剩下的。「買了多少」不是費用，「用掉多少」才是。

    Example: supplies on hand at the start of the month €1,500; €2,800 more bought for cash; €1,100 on hand at month-end. (a) What is supplies expense? (b) If the adjusting entry is forgotten, what is misstated?

    Solution 解答

    (a) 1,500 + 2,800 − 1,100 = €3,200. Entry: Dr Supplies Expense 3,200 / Cr Supplies 3,200.

    (b) Expenses are understated by €3,200, so net income and equity are overstated by €3,200, and Supplies (assets) are overstated by €3,200.忘了調整：費用少記 → 淨利、權益高估；用品（資產）也高估，全部是同一個金額。

    Trap: Same transaction, mirror image. Payer: Prepaid (asset) → Expense. Receiver: Unearned (liability) → Revenue.付款方用預付（資產），收款方用預收（負債）。

    05Recording Process, Trial Balance, Closing, SFP & Ratios

    ConceptThe accounting information system

    An accounting information system identifies, records, and communicates the economic events of an organization to interested users. External users (investors, creditors, tax authorities) use financial accounting; internal users (managers) use managerial accounting.會計資訊系統：辨認 → 記錄 → 溝通。外部使用者看財務會計，內部使用者看管理會計。

    Not every activity is a transaction: first ask whether the event changes the financial position (assets, liabilities, or equity). Hiring an employee or signing a contract for future work is not recorded yet.先判斷有沒有改變財務狀況；雇用員工、簽約但尚未履行都不入帳。

    StepsThe recording process

    1. Analyze each transaction from business documents: does it exist, which accounts, how much to debit/credit? 分析交易
    2. Journalize: enter it in the journal (date; debit account first on the left; credit account indented below). 記日記帳
    3. Post: transfer journal entries to the ledger accounts in chronological order. 過帳到分類帳
    4. Prepare the trial balance from the ledger balances. 編試算表

    DefinitionsTrial balance and its limitations

    A trial balance lists accounts and their balances at a given time, in ledger order, in two columns (debit and credit). It proves that total debits = total credits, may uncover journalizing/posting errors, and is used to prepare financial statements.試算表驗證借貸相等，可以抓到部分錯誤，也是編報表的基礎。

    Trap — the trial balance still balances when:
    1. a transaction is not journalized at all;
    2. a correct journal entry is not posted;
    3. a journal entry is posted twice;
    4. incorrect accounts are used in journalizing or posting (e.g., debit Supplies instead of Equipment);
    5. offsetting errors are made in recording the amount.
    只要借貸「同時」錯（漏記、重複記、記錯科目、互相抵銷的錯誤），試算表照樣平衡。只有借貸一邊錯（例如只過了借方，或借方金額打錯）才會不平衡。

    FormatClassified statement of financial position (IFRS order)

    Assets (least liquid first)

    • Intangible assets
    • Property, plant, and equipment
    • Long-term investments
    • Current assets (inventory, prepaid, receivables, cash)

    Equity and liabilities

    • Equity (share capital—ordinary, retained earnings)
    • Non-current liabilities
    • Current liabilities

    IFRS 的排法：非流動資產在前、流動資產在後；權益在前、負債在後（跟考題 Bikes-Are-Us 的格式一樣）。

    RatiosThree families of ratios

    FamilyMeasuresExamples

    FormatMultiple-step income statement

    Sales Revenue 銷貨收入
      − Sales Returns and Allowances 銷貨退回與折讓
      − Sales Discounts 銷貨折扣

      − Cost of Goods Sold 銷貨成本
    = Gross Profit 毛利
      − Operating Expenses (salaries, rent, freight-out, insurance…) 營業費用
    = Income from Operations 營業利益
      ± Other Income and Expense 其他收益及費損

    Beginning Inventory
    + Purchases
      − Purchase Returns and Allowances
      − Purchase Discounts

    − Ending Inventory
    = Cost of Goods Sold

    07Inventory: Freight Terms, Ownership & Count Adjustments

    DefinitionsShipping terms

    FOB Shipping PointFOB / CIF Destination
    Ownership passes 所有權轉移When goods leave the seller (placed with the carrier)When goods reach the buyer
    Who pays freight 誰付運費Buyer → Freight-in, part of inventory costSeller → Freight-out, an operating expense
    Goods in transit at year-end 年底在途Belong to the buyerBelong to the seller

    TA reminder: freight cost is about who bears the cost; ownership is a separate question.

    RulesWhat goes into inventory?

    INCLUDE 計入

    • Goods purchased FOB shipping point, still in transit
    • Goods sold FOB destination, still in transit
    • Your goods out on consignment (you are the consignor)
    • Goods still on your loading dock, not yet shipped
    在途的 FOB 起運點進貨、在途的 FOB 目的地銷貨、我方寄出去的寄銷品、還沒出貨的貨。

    EXCLUDE 排除

    • Goods held on consignment for others (you are the consignee)
    • Office supplies — not inventory held for resale
    • Obsolete goods: IFRS requires lower-of-cost-or-net realizable value (LCNRV); write down to zero if no other use
    • Goods already shipped to customers FOB shipping point
    別人寄放的寄銷品、辦公用品、過時存貨（成本與淨變現價值孰低）、已出貨的 FOB 起運點銷貨。

    Ask two questions for each item: (1) Does Sergei own it on Dec 31? (FOB terms, consignment) (2) Is it inventory held for resale, and at what value? (supplies, LCNRV)每項問自己兩件事：12/31 這是不是 Sergei 的？它是不是待售存貨、該用多少金額？

    DefinitionsPeriodic vs. perpetual

    08Inventory Costing: FIFO, LIFO, Average-cost

    ConceptOne cost pool, split two ways

    The cost of goods available for sale (GAFS) is fixed. The costing method only changes how that pool is split between ending inventory (statement of financial position) and COGS (income statement). So Ending Inventory + COGS = GAFS under every method — use it to check your work.成本法只決定怎麼切同一包成本；期末存貨 + COGS 永遠 = 可售商品成本。

    FIFO (first-in, first-out)

    Oldest units are sold first → ending inventory = most recent purchases. Count backward from the latest batch.先進先出：期末存貨是最新的幾批。

    LIFO (last-in, first-out)

    Newest units are sold first → ending inventory = oldest costs. Count forward from beginning inventory. (Not permitted under IFRS, but tested.)後進先出：期末存貨是最舊的。IFRS 不允許但會考。

    Average-cost

    Weighted-average unit cost = GAFS cost ÷ total units available. Ending inventory = units on hand × average cost.加權平均：總成本 ÷ 總數量，不是單價直接平均。

    In a period of rising prices: FIFO → highest ending inventory, lowest COGS, highest net income. LIFO → the opposite. Average-cost is in between.物價上漲：FIFO 淨利最高，LIFO 最低。

    TaxesWhich method keeps more cash? (2025 Part B)

    Cost-flow methods change COGS, so they change taxable income and the income tax actually paid. When prices are rising, LIFO charges the newest, most expensive costs to COGS → lowest income before tax → lowest income tax → most cash kept. FIFO gives the highest net income but the highest tax. LIFO is not allowed under IFRS, but exams still ask you to compute it.物價上漲：LIFO 淨利最低、稅最少、現金留最多；FIFO 淨利最高、稅最多、期末存貨最接近現時成本。

    MethodPeriodic ending inventory

    1. List each layer (units × unit cost); total GAFS units and cost.列出每一批，加總數量與成本。
    2. Units in ending inventory = units available − units sold (or given).期末數量 = 可售 − 賣出。
    3. FIFO: take from the latest layer backward. LIFO: take from the beginning layer forward.FIFO 從最後一批拿；LIFO 從期初拿。
    4. COGS = GAFS − ending inventory.

    ExampleMoncada Stores

    Moncada made three purchases: (1) 200 units at £7, (2) 300 units at £8, (3) 150 units at £9. It uses a periodic system. With 220 units on hand, compute ending inventory under (a) FIFO and (b) average-cost.

    詳解 Solution

    (a) FIFO — the 220 units left are the latest purchases: all 150 from purchase (3) at £9 = 1,350, plus 70 more from purchase (2) at £8 = 560 → £1,910

    (b) Average — GAFS = 200 × 7 + 300 × 8 + 150 × 9 = 1,400 + 2,400 + 1,350 = £5,150 for 650 units → £5,150 ÷ 650 = £7.92; 220 × 7.92 = £1,742.40

    平均單價是 7.92，不是 (7+8+9)÷3 = 8，因為每批數量不同，要加權。

    ExampleTinker Bell Magic Shop

    Inventory Jan 1: 8,000 units at £11; Purchase June 19: 13,000 at £12; Purchase Nov 8: 5,000 at £13. With 9,000 units on hand at Dec 31, what is ending inventory under FIFO? Under LIFO?

    詳解 Solution

    FIFO — take from the newest layer back: 5,000 × £13 = 65,000 (Nov 8, all of it) + 4,000 × £12 = 48,000 (June 19) → £113,000 (answer c)

    LIFO — take from the oldest layer forward: 8,000 × £11 = 88,000 (Jan 1, all of it) + 1,000 × £12 = 12,000 (June 19) → £100,000 (answer d)

    FIFO 期末留下「最新」的貨；LIFO 期末留下「最舊」的貨。物價上漲時 FIFO 期末存貨較高。

    Incoterms® (published by the ICC since 1936) set when ownership and risk transfer and who pays freight, taxes, and insurance.     Merchandising company

    DefinitionsCredit terms

    11Accounts Receivable & Bad Debts

    DefinitionsTypes of receivables

    Receivables are amounts due from individuals and companies that are expected to be collected in cash.應收款項：預期會收到現金的債權。

    Accounts receivable 應收帳款

    Amounts customers owe on account from the sale of goods and services. Usually 30–60 days; usually the most significant type.

    Notes receivable 應收票據

    A written promise (formal instrument) for an amount to be received. Usually 60–90 days or longer; involves interest. A/R and notes receivable from sales are called trade receivables.

    Other receivables 其他應收款

    Non-trade receivables: interest, loans to officers, advances to employees, income taxes refundable. They do not result from the operations of the business.

    DefinitionsValuing receivables

    Valuation
    Determine the net amount of cash the company expects to receive (net realizable value) and report it in the financial statements.評價：以預期可收現金（淨變現價值）表達。
    Uncollectible accounts
    Sales on account raise the possibility of accounts not being collected — a normal risk of doing business. Uncollectible amounts are classified as an operating expense (Bad Debt Expense).收不回的帳款列營業費用。
    Doubtful debt vs. bad debt
    Doubtful: money you predict will be uncollectible. Bad debt: debt you have officially written off.doubtful 是預估可能收不回；bad debt 是正式沖銷。

    Direct Write-off Method

    Losses are recorded when accounts actually cannot be collected. Simple, but generally not acceptable for financial reporting.直接沖銷法：確定收不回才認列，不被財報接受。

    Allowance Method

    Losses are estimated: matches estimated uncollectibles with revenues in the same period; receivables stated at cash (net) realizable value. Required by IFRS.備抵法：期末估計，費用與收入同期，IFRS 要求。

    ConceptDepreciation

    Depreciation allocates the cost of a tangible asset to expense over its useful life in a rational and systematic way (expense recognition / matching). It is cost allocation, not asset valuation: book value (cost − accumulated depreciation) is not fair value. Land is not depreciated.     (Cost − Residual) ÷ Life = 12,000 ÷ 5 = €2,400 per year (rate 20%). Same every year; suits buildings, furniture; most common.

    Orchid Bakery bought an oven on April 1, 2025 for $84,000. Residual value $4,000; useful life 5 years or 20,000 working hours; year-end December 31. Compute: (a) straight-line depreciation for 2025; (b) units-of-activity depreciation for 2025 if the oven ran 3,500 hours; (c) double-declining-balance depreciation for 2025 and 2026.

    詳解 Solution

    (a) (84,000 − 4,000) ÷ 5 = 16,000 per year × 9/12 = $12,000 (April–December = 9 months)

    (b) (84,000 − 4,000) ÷ 20,000 = $4 per hour × 3,500 = $14,000 (units-of-activity needs no month fraction — the hours already measure the use)

    (c) Rate 2 × 1/5 = 40%. 2025: 84,000 × 40% × 9/12 = $25,200. 2026: (84,000 − 25,200) × 40% = $23,520 — a full year on the new book value.

    只有買進那一年要乘月份比例；倍數餘額遞減法第二年用「期初帳面價值」算整年。

On December 31, 2024, before adjusting entries, it changed the remaining estimated life to 3 years (including 2024) and the residual value to £6,000. What is depreciation expense for 2024?

    詳解 Solution

    Old depreciation = (90,000 − 10,000) ÷ 8 = 10,000 per year. 2021–2023: 3 years × 10,000 = 30,000.

    Book value at January 1, 2024 = 90,000 − 30,000 = 60,000. New depreciable amount = 60,000 − 6,000 = 54,000.

    2024 depreciation = 54,000 ÷ 3 = £18,000.

    13Current & Non-current Liabilities, Bonds

    DefinitionsCurrent vs. non-current

    Liabilities are existing debts and obligations settled in the future by transferring assets/services or creating other liabilities. Maturity ≤ 1 year → current; > 1 year → non-current. Classification matters for liquidity (short-term debt-paying ability) and for the order of payment in bankruptcy (debt holders first, then preferred shareholders, then ordinary shareholders).一年內到期是流動負債；破產清算時債權人優先受償。

    Current liabilities include accounts payable, notes payable, sales taxes payable, interest payable, unearned revenues, and accrued liabilities (taxes, salaries and wages). The part of long-term debt due within one year is shown as current maturities of long-term debt (Apple splits its term debt into current 12,350 and non-current 78,328).長期負債一年內到期的部分要轉列流動負債。

    Marlow Corp's adjusted trial balance at December 31 includes the liabilities below. Classify each one, then total the current and non-current liabilities.

    詳解 Solution

    Current = interest 36,000 + 6-month note 70,000 + mortgage due within a year 90,000 + salaries 22,000 + unearned revenue 18,000 + income taxes 41,000 = $277,000.

    Non-current = bonds 900,000 + 4-year note 250,000 + rest of the mortgage 510,000 = $1,660,000.

    判斷標準只有一個：一年內要付掉的就是流動。抵押借款要拆成兩部分。

    EntriesNotes payable

    A written promissory note, usually interest-bearing. Three steps: (1) record the liability at face value; (2) accrue interest = Principal × Rate × Time; (3) at maturity pay face value + interest.應付票據：利息＝本金 × 利率 × 期間。

    Cash received before the revenue is earned is a liability; as the performance obligation is satisfied it becomes revenue (Ch3 adjusting entry).     DefinitionsBonds (non-current liabilities)

    A bond is a form of interest-bearing note used to raise large amounts of long-term capital. Key features: face value (principal), maturity date, contractual (stated) interest rate (cash interest = face × contractual rate; often paid semiannually).公司債：面額、到期日、票面利率。

    Contractual rate vs market rateBonds sell at
    Contractual > marketPremium (above face value) 溢價

    Total cost of borrowing = cash interest paid + discount (or − premium).     2. Must repay principal at maturity

    Not suitable for companies with fluctuating earnings and a weak cash position.

    15Internal Control, Cash & the Statement of Cash Flows

    DefinitionsCash, cash equivalents, restricted cash

    Cash equivalents are short-term, highly liquid investments that are (1) readily convertible to known amounts of cash and (2) so near maturity that their market value is relatively insensitive to interest-rate changes. Restricted cash is not available for general use (set aside for a special purpose) and can be classified as non-current. Cash is reported on both the SFP and the statement of cash flows.約當現金：短期、高流動、到期日近、價值不太受利率影響。受限制現金不能自由動用，可列非流動資產。

    Internal control is the set of methods a company uses to safeguard its assets, make its accounting records reliable, increase efficiency, and ensure compliance with laws. Fraud needs three things (the fraud triangle): opportunity, financial pressure, and rationalization. Internal control mainly removes the opportunity.內部控制：保護資產、讓會計紀錄可靠、提升效率、遵守法規。舞弊三角：機會、壓力、合理化；內控主要是消除「機會」。

    ConceptsNet income vs operating cash flow (2025 Part B)

    Net loss, yet positive operating cash flow?

    Yes. (1) Non-cash expenses — depreciation, amortization, losses on disposal — reduce net income but use no cash, so they are added back. (2) Working capital can release cash: collecting more receivables than this year's sales (A/R ↓), selling inventory bought earlier (inventory ↓), or not yet paying expenses (A/P ↑).淨損但營業現金流量為正：非現金費用加回＋營運資金變動帶進現金。

    Which better reflects performance?

    Accrual net income matches revenues and expenses to the period, so it better measures profitability and long-term performance. Operating cash flow better shows liquidity — the ability to pay bills, repay debt and reinvest — and is harder to shape with estimates. Look at both; a large, persistent gap is a warning sign.看獲利能力用淨利；看流動性、付款能力用營業現金流量。兩個都要看。
